# Code of Conduct

OpenMascot adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge (summary)

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and
kindness, respect for differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting
constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on
what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour: sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or
private harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit
permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

The full text — including scope, enforcement responsibilities and the
enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) —
is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and
applies to this project in its entirety.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers by opening
a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/jbraunschweiger/openmascot/security/advisories/new)
(visible only to maintainers) or by contacting a maintainer directly. All
reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy of the reporter will be respected.
